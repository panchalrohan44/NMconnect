Privacy Policy

How SVKM Connect handles your data

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you use SVKM Connect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By registering or using the service you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and sign-in method (email + password or Google account). We never see or store your password; it is handled by our authentication provider.
  • Profile information — your display name, age, gender, college, course, year, religion, mother tongue, favourite hangout, hobbies and interests, clubs/fests/events, and a short bio.
  • Social handles — Instagram, Snapchat, or other handles you choose to share on your profile.
  • Activity — connection requests you send or receive and their status.
  • Technical data — basic device and browser information needed for the site to work.

2. How we collect it

You provide most of this information yourself when you create an account and complete your profile. When you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address with us (their use is governed by Google's privacy policy). We do not buy profile data from anyone.

3. How we use it

  • Show verified SVKM student profiles to other members of the community.
  • Match students based on the filters and preferences you choose (college, course, year, gender, hangout, interests).
  • Let you send and receive connection requests.
  • Keep the community safe by verifying members are real students aged 18+.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored with our cloud provider (Supabase) which encrypts data in transit and at rest. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes by our authentication provider and are never readable by us. Access to profiles is restricted through row-level security so that only signed-in community members can view them, and each user can only modify their own profile.

5. Who we share it with

  • Your profile is visible to other verified, signed-in SVKM student members. It is never shown publicly or to search engines.
  • Google, when you choose the "Continue with Google" sign-in option (for authentication only).
  • Our hosting/authentication provider (Supabase) as the technical platform.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or any third party.

6. Your choices and control

  • Edit or remove details from your profile at any time.
  • Sign out from any device.
  • Request that your profile and related data be deleted by contacting us (see section 10).

7. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to remember your sign-in session and preferences. Google may set its own cookies when you choose "Continue with Google" — please see Google's cookie and privacy policies for details. These technologies do not let us access other sites you visit.

8. Age requirement

SVKM Connect is only for people aged 18 and older. By signing up you confirm you are at least 18. If we learn a user is under 18, we will remove their account and data.

9. Children's privacy

The service is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or requests to delete your data can be sent to panchalrohan005@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be visible on this page.
