About SVKM Connect

Made for better campus connections.

SVKM Connect gives students a simple way to discover compatible people, start conversations, and build meaningful connections across campus.

Explore matches How it works

Our purpose

Starting a conversation should not feel complicated. SVKM Connect brings campus discovery, preferences, and connection requests into one focused experience.

The project is designed for the SVKM student community, with profile choices that help people find others who share their college, course, interests, and campus experience.

Every account is tied to a real student identity, and demo profiles are always labelled BOT so you always know what you are looking at.

What you can do

Every part of the experience is designed to help you move from discovery to a real conversation without unnecessary steps.

01

Set your preferences

Choose your college, course, year, and the kind of people you would like to meet.

02

Discover possible matches

Browse every compatible profile available from the connected student database.

03

Start a connection

Send a connection request and unlock private chat when the other student accepts.

How possible matches work

The live Possible Matches panel checks every available profile and applies the same clear criteria each time.

Same campus course

Your course must match the profile course.

Same college

Your college must match the profile college.

Any year

Profiles from every year qualify, with your own year listed first when available.

When your profile lists a gender, complementary, non-binary, open, or unspecified profiles may appear. If no gender is specified, the results include every matching gender.

If your branch has fewer than four students signed up yet, the panel is topped up with clearly labelled campus bot profiles so you always have something to look at while the community grows.

What we value

A respectful campus experience depends on clear choices, considerate communication, and responsible use of everyone's information.

Student control

You decide what to share, who you connect with, and when you want to move a conversation forward.

Respectful discovery

Profiles are intended for kind, genuine, and consent-based campus interactions.

Transparent matching

The live panel explains the criteria used and does not hide additional results behind a short preview.
